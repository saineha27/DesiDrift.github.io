✦ Local Flavours

Eat Like a Local,
Everywhere You Go

India's cuisine is as diverse as its landscape. Every state has a dish that'll make your tastebuds drift.

🍛
Butter Chicken
Punjab · North India
The world's most loved Indian dish — velvety tomato gravy with tender chicken.
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️
🦞
Fish Curry
Goa · West Coast
Tangy coconut-based curry with fresh catch — eaten with steamed rice by the sea.
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️
🥘
Dal Baati Churma
Rajasthan · Northwest
Hard wheat rolls dunked in rich lentils with a sweet crumble — the taste of the desert.
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️
🍜
Thukpa
Ladakh · Himalayas
A warming Tibetan noodle soup perfect for sub-zero nights in the mountains.
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️
🍲
Hyderabadi Biryani
Telangana · South
Slow-cooked basmati and spiced meat sealed in a clay pot — a Nizam-era legacy.
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️
🍱
Sadya
Kerala · South
A grand vegetarian feast on banana leaf with 26+ dishes — eaten with your hands.
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️
🥟
Momos
Sikkim · Northeast
Steamed dumplings stuffed with spiced veggies or meat. The streets of Gangtok smell of these.
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️
🍡
Pani Puri
Pan-India · Streetside
Crispy shells burst with spiced tamarind water. India's most universally loved street snack.
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️
